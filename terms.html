FRIEND BETA

Beta Terms

Last updated September 9, 2026

VERAMOR is currently a limited testing experience. By participating, you agree to use the beta only for lawful, respectful dating and product-testing purposes and to follow these beta rules.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and must create only your own profile. Do not impersonate another person or submit deceptive, stolen, or artificially generated identity media as if it were you.

Profile requirements

A profile must include the required basic information, at least four profile photos, and a private face-verification video before it can be submitted for review. Approval is not guaranteed, and a profile may be paused or removed when verification or safety concerns arise.

Conduct

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, discriminate against, exploit, scam, solicit, spam, or pressure other beta users. Do not post unlawful content, non-consensual intimate material, commercial sexual solicitation, or content involving minors.

Matches and messaging

A match does not create any obligation to meet, reply, continue a conversation, or share personal information. Messaging unlocks only after both people complete the Chemistry Check. Either person may unmatch or block at any time.

Beta service

The beta may change, pause, reset, or contain bugs. Features and data may be modified as the product is tested. Paid features are not active during this friend beta unless VERAMOR clearly states otherwise.

Removal

VERAMOR may limit, pause, or remove access for safety, abuse, fraud, testing, or rule violations. You may delete your own account from the beta settings.

Personal safety

Use judgment when meeting someone from the internet. Keep early meetings public, arrange your own transportation, tell someone you trust where you are going, and do not send money or financial credentials to another user.
