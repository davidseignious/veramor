FRIEND BETA

Beta Terms

Last updated September 9, 2026

VERAMOR is currently a limited testing experience. By participating, you agree to use the beta only for lawful, respectful dating and product-testing purposes and to follow these beta rules.

Eligibility

You must be between 18 and 100 years old and must create only your own profile. Do not impersonate another person or submit deceptive, stolen, or artificially generated identity media as if it were you.

Profile requirements

A profile must include the required basic information, at least four profile photos, and a private face-verification video before it can be submitted for review. Approval is not guaranteed, and a profile may be paused or removed when verification or safety concerns arise. Optional profile prompts may be answered with text, voice, or video.

Conduct

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, discriminate against, exploit, scam, solicit, spam, or pressure other beta users. Do not post unlawful content, non-consensual intimate material, commercial sexual solicitation, or content involving minors.

Matches, messaging and calls

A match does not create any obligation to meet, reply, continue a conversation, accept a call, turn on a camera or microphone, or share personal information. Messaging, voice calls, video calls, and Watch Together unlock only after both people complete the Chemistry Check. Either person may decline a call, end a session, unmatch, or block at any time.

Watch Together and third-party services

Watch Together may synchronize controls for supported embedded media or provide a shared countdown for third-party streaming services. VERAMOR does not grant access to paid media, bypass subscriptions, copy movies or shows, or provide shared streaming credentials. Each participant is responsible for lawful access to third-party content and for following the third party’s terms.

Beta service

The beta may change, pause, reset, or contain bugs. Calls may not connect on every network during beta testing. Features and data may be modified as the product is tested. Paid features are not active during this friend beta unless VERAMOR clearly states otherwise.

Removal

VERAMOR may limit, pause, or remove access for safety, abuse, fraud, testing, or rule violations. You may delete your own account from the beta settings.

Personal safety

Use judgment when meeting someone from the internet. Keep early meetings public, arrange your own transportation, tell someone you trust where you are going, and do not send money or financial credentials to another user.
