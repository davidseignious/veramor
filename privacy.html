BETA PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated September 9, 2026

VERAMOR’s friend beta collects the information needed to create, verify, display, and operate a dating profile. This can include your email address, profile details, photos, optional public intro video, private face-verification video, preferences, likes, matches, messages, blocks, reports, and basic activity records.

Face verification

The required face-verification video is stored separately from public profile media and is not displayed as part of your dating profile. During this beta it may be reviewed by the beta administrator to confirm that the profile appears to represent a real adult and that the submitted media is consistent with the account.

Who can see profile information

Your dating profile is kept out of discovery until it meets the beta requirements and is approved. Once live, other verified beta users may see the profile information and media you chose for your dating profile. Blocks are used to prevent blocked accounts from seeing or interacting with one another through discovery.

Messages and safety reports

Messages are available only to active matches after both people complete the Chemistry Check. Reports may be reviewed for safety and moderation. Do not put highly sensitive information in profile text, messages, or reports.

Account deletion

The beta includes an in-app account deletion control. When deletion completes, VERAMOR removes the account and its stored profile, profile video, and verification media from the beta systems. Records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or system integrity may be handled separately if required.

Beta limitations

This is a limited friend beta and is still being tested. Features, retention practices, and this notice may change before a public launch. Do not use the beta if you are under 18.

Questions

For beta privacy or safety questions, contact the VERAMOR beta administrator through the invitation channel you received.
