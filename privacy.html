BETA PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Version 2026-09-11.1 · Last updated September 11, 2026

This notice explains how VERAMOR's Friend Beta handles personal information used to create, verify, display, match, message, call, plan dates, share profiles, and provide safety features. The Friend Beta is a limited U.S. testing service and is not yet a public international launch.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, VERAMOR may process your email address, account identifiers, date of birth, profile details, city and approximate location information, relationship and discovery preferences, photos, optional public intro video, text/voice/video prompt answers, private face-verification video, likes, skips, stronger interest signals, matches, Chemistry Check completion, messages, date plans, call-session and connection metadata, Watch Together room state, blocks, reports, notification preferences, profile-share link state, optional connected-service information, device/browser information, and security/activity records.

How we use information

We use information to operate the beta, verify profile readiness, show profiles to eligible members, apply discovery preferences, create connections, deliver messages and calls, coordinate Watch Together and date-planning features, provide account and safety controls, detect abuse, investigate reports, improve reliability, comply with legal obligations, and protect VERAMOR and its users.

Face verification and biometrics

The Friend Beta currently uses a private front-facing verification video for identity/liveness review and profile consistency checks. The verification video is stored separately from public profile media and is not shown on your dating profile. In the current Friend Beta, VERAMOR does not intentionally create a face-geometry template, voiceprint, or automated facial-recognition profile from this verification video. If VERAMOR later introduces technology that creates regulated biometric identifiers or templates, the product must provide any additional notice and consent required before that processing begins.

Profile prompts and public content

Profile photos, public intro video, selected profile details, interests, and prompt answers are visible to other eligible users according to the product's discovery and connection rules. Prompt media is stored separately from private verification media. Do not upload content you do not have permission to use.

Messages, reports, and moderation

Messages are available only to active connections after the Chemistry Check requirements are satisfied. Reports and related content may be reviewed for safety, fraud prevention, moderation, legal compliance, or enforcement. Reports involving suspected minors, threats, scams, or apparent child sexual exploitation may receive elevated review and may be preserved or reported when required by law.

Calls

Voice and video calls use browser real-time communication and peer-to-peer media when available. VERAMOR stores limited call and signaling metadata needed to request, connect, manage, and troubleshoot the session. VERAMOR does not provide a call-recording feature. Camera and microphone access require device permission.

Watch Together and streaming accounts

For supported YouTube playback, VERAMOR uses YouTube API/embedded-player services to coordinate supported playback behavior while YouTube serves the media. For Netflix, Max, Hulu, Disney+, Prime Video, and similar providers, each person opens the provider using their own authorized account. VERAMOR does not ask for, receive, or store those streaming passwords and does not rebroadcast third-party movies or shows.

Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Connected services

If you choose to connect an eligible third-party account such as Instagram, VERAMOR may process the access token and the account/media information needed for that optional feature. Access tokens are kept out of the public profile. Disconnecting removes the active VERAMOR connection and synced feature records as supported by the beta.

Service providers

VERAMOR relies on infrastructure and service providers to host the application, database, authentication, storage, media, communications, and deployment. Current beta infrastructure includes services such as Supabase, Vercel, and Cloudinary, along with third-party services you choose to use such as YouTube or Instagram. Providers may process information only as needed to provide their services subject to their applicable agreements and policies.

Sale, advertising, and AI training

VERAMOR does not sell Friend Beta personal information as a product feature and does not use private verification video or private messages to train a general-purpose third-party AI model as part of the current Friend Beta. If those practices materially change, this notice and the applicable consent flow must be updated before the new use begins.

Retention

Account and profile information is generally retained while the beta account remains active. Messages, safety records, connection records, and operational logs may be retained as needed to provide the service, investigate abuse, prevent fraud, enforce rules, resolve disputes, maintain system integrity, or comply with law. Private verification media is retained while needed for beta verification and account-safety purposes unless removed earlier. Account deletion initiates removal of account-linked beta data supported by the deletion workflow; limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or backup integrity.

Security

VERAMOR uses technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to a private beta, including authenticated access, restricted storage paths, signed media access where supported, and product-level access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can edit profile information, disable discovery, disconnect optional integrations, block or report users, adjust available notification/call settings, revoke a profile-share link, and delete your account through the beta controls. You may also stop using the beta at any time.

Age and geography

VERAMOR is for adults 18 and older. The current Friend Beta is intended for U.S. testing. International Browse may show profiles from outside your local area, but that feature does not by itself mean VERAMOR has launched account registration in every country or accepted all international legal regimes.

Changes

If VERAMOR materially changes how it handles personal information, the notice version will be updated. Where required, users will be asked to accept the revised notice before continuing with the affected processing.

Questions and requests

During the private Friend Beta, privacy, safety, copyright, and account requests may be submitted through the beta invitation or administrator channel provided to the tester. A dedicated public legal/privacy contact and operating-entity notice must be published before a broad public launch.
