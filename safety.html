VERAMOR DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND SCREENINGS ON ITS MEMBERS.

VERAMOR is 18+ only. Profile and face-video verification are safety tools, but verification is not a criminal background check and does not guarantee another person's identity, history, intentions, or behavior.

Core safety notice

There is no substitute for acting with caution when communicating with a stranger who wants to meet you.

Do not include your home address, personal phone number, place of work, financial information, passwords, one-time codes, or other unnecessary identifying information in your public profile or early messages. Stop communicating with anyone who pressures you for personal or financial information or tries to trick you into revealing it.

If you meet another member in person, tell someone you trust where you are going and when you expect to return. Arrange your own transportation and meet in a public place with other people around.

Before meeting

Keep early conversation in VERAMOR when possible. Never send money, gift cards, banking credentials, account passwords, authentication codes, intimate images under pressure, or identifying documents to another user. Be cautious if someone quickly pressures you to move to another messaging service.

Voice and video calls

Only accept calls you want. A connection never creates an obligation to turn on your camera or microphone. Calls are not recorded by VERAMOR as a product feature. Do not record or distribute another person's call without their consent. If a call becomes uncomfortable, end it and use block or report.

Watch Together

Watch Together is for shared viewing between consenting connections. Each person must use their own authorized streaming account. VERAMOR does not ask for or store Netflix, Max, Hulu, Disney+, Prime Video, or other streaming passwords. Do not share streaming credentials, payment information, or pirated streams.

Suspected minors and child sexual exploitation

VERAMOR is strictly 18+. Immediately report any account you believe belongs to a minor. Never request, create, send, save, or redistribute sexual content involving a person under 18. Reports involving apparent child sexual exploitation are treated as urgent safety matters and may be preserved or reported when required by law.

Use block and report

Block when you do not want further contact. Report impersonation, scams, threats, harassment, stalking, solicitation, suspected minors, non-consensual sexual content, child sexual exploitation concerns, or other unsafe behavior. Blocking ends the connection in the beta.

Consent

A connection, Chemistry Check answer, message, call, Watch Together session, date, or prior relationship never implies ongoing consent. Respect boundaries and stop when asked.

Urgent danger

VERAMOR is not an emergency service. If there is an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
