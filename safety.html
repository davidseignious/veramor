SAFETY FIRST

Friend Beta Safety Rules

VERAMOR is 18+ only. Verification reduces risk; it does not guarantee another person’s intentions or behavior.

Before meeting

Keep early conversation in the app when possible. Do not send money, banking details, passwords, one-time codes, intimate images, or identifying documents to another user. Be cautious if someone pressures you to move immediately to another messaging service.

First meetings

Meet in a public place, arrange your own transportation, tell someone you trust where you will be, and keep control of your food and drinks. Leave if you feel unsafe or pressured.

Use block and report

Block immediately when you do not want further contact. Report profiles or behavior involving impersonation, scams, threats, harassment, solicitation, suspected minors, non-consensual sexual content, or other safety concerns. Blocking ends the connection in the beta.

Consent

A match, Chemistry Check answer, message, date, or prior relationship never implies ongoing consent. Respect boundaries and stop when asked.

Urgent danger

VERAMOR is not an emergency service. If there is an immediate threat to someone’s safety, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
