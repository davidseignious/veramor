Copyright & Takedown

Last updated September 11, 2026

VERAMOR users may upload only photos, videos, audio, text, and other content they own or have permission to use. VERAMOR may remove content that appears to infringe another person's rights and may restrict or terminate repeat infringers.

Submitting an infringement notice

During the private Friend Beta, send copyright complaints through the beta administrator or invitation channel supplied to you. A complete notice should identify the copyrighted work, identify the material you believe infringes it and where it appears, provide your contact information, include a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized, include a statement that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the owner, and include your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notice

If your content is removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe the removal was mistaken, contact the beta administrator and identify the removed material, explain the basis for your good-faith belief that removal was a mistake, and provide the information reasonably necessary to evaluate the dispute.

Repeat infringement

VERAMOR may suspend or terminate accounts that repeatedly upload infringing material or misuse the reporting process.

Public-launch requirement

The Friend Beta process above does not represent that VERAMOR has completed every requirement for federal DMCA safe-harbor protection. Before broad public launch with user uploads, VERAMOR must publish the operating entity and designated-agent contact information and complete any required U.S. Copyright Office designated-agent registration.
